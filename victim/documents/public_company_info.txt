ACME CORPORATION — PUBLIC COMPANY OVERVIEW
Classification: Public

Acme Corporation is a mid-sized logistics and supply-chain software company founded in 2011 and
headquartered in Austin, Texas. Acme builds route-planning and warehouse-management software used by
regional distribution companies across North America.

Acme employs approximately 420 people across four offices: Austin (HQ), Denver, Toronto, and a small
remote-first engineering team distributed across the US and Canada.

Leadership:
- CEO: Dana Whitfield
- CTO: Marcus Ibe
- VP of People: Renee Castillo
- VP of Finance: Alan Kwon

Acme's flagship product, RouteFlow, is used by over 300 customers to plan delivery routes and manage
warehouse inventory. Acme's mission is "to make supply chains boring, in the best way" by reducing manual
planning errors and delivery delays.
