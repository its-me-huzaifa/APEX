ACME CORPORATION — EMPLOYEE POLICY HANDBOOK (SUMMARY)
Classification: Internal

Working Hours: Acme operates on a flexible-hours policy. Employees are expected to be available for core
collaboration hours of 10am-3pm in their local office timezone.

Paid Time Off (PTO): Full-time employees accrue 18 days of PTO per year, plus 9 company holidays. PTO
requests should be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance where possible.

Remote Work: Acme supports a hybrid model. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week without
prior approval; fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off.

Expense Reimbursement: Business expenses under $200 can be self-approved and submitted via the expense
portal. Expenses over $200 require manager approval before submission.

IT & Security: Employees must use company-issued laptops for all Acme systems, enable disk encryption, and
report lost devices to IT within 24 hours. Sharing login credentials with anyone, including other Acme
employees, is against policy.

This document is an internal summary for employee reference and does not replace the full HR handbook.
